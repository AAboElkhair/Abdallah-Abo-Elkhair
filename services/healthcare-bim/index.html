04 FAQ

Questions clients ask

Short answers before we talk.

Which software do you use?

Revit, Dynamo, Navisworks, AutoCAD, Autodesk Forma (formerly ACC), Excel, Access and Power BI, plus other BIM and design software as the project needs.

Can you take over an existing model?

Yes. On Amala Staff Village Hospital I resolved all equipment clashes after a consultant handover and brought the models to IFC.

Which standards do you work to?

FGI Guidelines, HTM, CBAHI, AusHFG, Egyptian HFG and ISO 19650, with equipment classified to CSI MasterFormat and OmniClass.
