06 FAQ

Questions clients ask

Short answers before we talk.

What deliverables do you provide?

RBR schedules and room data sheets, room layout sheets, Shop Drawings (LOD 400) and As-Built drawings (LOD 500), technical specifications, BOQs and cost estimates, and pre-installation / MEP requirements.

Which standards do you work to?

FGI Guidelines, HTM, CBAHI, AusHFG, Egyptian HFG and ISO 19650, with equipment classified to CSI MasterFormat and OmniClass.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Cairo and have planned projects remotely for teams in Australia and Saudi Arabia.
