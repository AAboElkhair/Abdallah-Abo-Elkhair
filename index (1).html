What I do

Planning that hospitals can build from, and models that stay useful after handover.

Medical equipment planning

  • Equipment lists and schedules by department and room
  • Power, medical gas, data, water and drainage needs per item
  • Layouts and clearances checked against how each room is used
  • Coordination with architects and MEP teams

Healthcare BIM

  • Revit models from concept to LOD 500
  • Equipment families with the parameters planners and contractors need
  • Clash coordination in Navisworks
  • Shared models managed to ISO 19650 in ACC / BIM 360

Standards and compliance

  • Designs checked against FGI guidelines
  • HTM technical memoranda for UK-style healthcare facilities
  • CBAHI accreditation requirements
  • Documentation that points back to the standard

Automation and reporting

  • Dynamo scripts for repetitive modelling work
  • Excel schedules and checks
  • Power BI dashboards for project data
