Questions

Who is A+ MQ for?

Medical equipment planners and BIM teams who plan equipment for hospitals in Revit.

Can I see a demo?

Yes. Send a demo request and include your team size. The demo covers the tools that are in daily use.

Is it available now?

Not yet. Six tools are in daily use and still being tested. Join the early-access list and you will hear first.

Will there be team and company licenses?

Yes. Team and company licenses are planned with a lower price per seat. Get in touch to discuss your team.

Can I suggest a tool?

Yes. The roadmap is shaped by planners' feedback. Send your suggestion by email or LinkedIn.

Is A+ MQ affiliated with Autodesk?

No. It is an independent product for use with Autodesk Revit.
