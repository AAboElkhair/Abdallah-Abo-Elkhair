Automating Repetitive Revit Work in Medical Equipment Planning: Introducing A+ MQ

11 November 2026 · 1 min read

RevitAutomationMedical equipment planningA+ MQ

On large hospital projects, a big share of my time as a Medical Equipment Planner goes into repetitive Revit work: which rooms are furnished, what is still missing, whether the datasheets are up to date, plus layout sheets and tags for every room.

Building Explorer was the first step. Today it is part of a full Revit plugin, A+ MQ, built straight out of my day-to-day medical equipment planning work.

Tools I already use every day

These tools are part of my daily work. I am still trying and testing them, and I plan to release them soon.

  • Building Explorer: room-by-room furnishing status across the building
  • Furnishing Report: furnished rooms and missing items
  • Equipment Datasheet: datasheets straight from the equipment database
  • Room Layout Sheet: a ready layout sheet for every room in one click
  • Room Tags Auto Leader: room tags with automatic leaders
  • Sheet Export: sheets exported to CAD or PDF in one click, no hassle

Ten more tools on the way

I am currently working on 10 more tools, from RDS Sync and RBR Schedule to Utility Matrix and Medical Gas Map. The full list in the infographic:

  • Healthcare QA: Item Code Guard
  • Planning: Smart Placement, RDS Sync, RBR Schedule, Equipment BOQ
  • Coordination: Utility Matrix, Medical Gas Map, Ceiling Load Map, Clearance Check
  • Sheets: As-Built Tracker

Your input

I would love your input: if one tool could save you the most time in medical equipment planning on Revit, what would it be? And what else should we automate to make our work faster? Tell me on LinkedIn.
